# Landing Page Visual Direction V1

**Status:** Visual identity definition for the future landing page — art direction only.  
**Date (UTC):** 2026-07-29  
**Governing authorities:** Landing Constitution · IA · Copy · Evidence · Storyboard · Visual Narrative · Wireframe  
**Brand authority:** `branding/cartflow_brand_foundation_v1/` (logo, palette, Tajawal) — colour/type **tokens** remain there; this pack does not redefine them.

**Non-goals:** No hi-fi Figma. No components. No frontend. No final screenshot selection. No 3D production. No precise colour/typography token inventing.

---

## 1. Desired emotional impression

After scrolling, the merchant should feel:

> This is a calm, serious product that shows me real operations — not a loud recovery gadget.

| Feel | Not feel |
|------|----------|
| Trust | Hype |
| Product maturity | Demo-day theatrics |
| Clarity | Confusion / clutter |
| Calm confidence | Urgency / scarcity |
| Operational intelligence | AI mystique |

---

## 2. Visual personality

CartFlow landing personality:

**Calm operational premium** — green-trust SaaS that proves itself with product surfaces.

Aligned with Brand Foundation: *Simple · Clean · Calm · Modern · Trustworthy* and *Understand → Decide → Act → Recover*.

Landing-specific emphasis (from Constitution + Visual Narrative):

- **Product-first** — screenshots and real UI outrank illustration  
- **Trust-first** — honesty states and whitespace beat decoration  
- **Story-earned intelligence** — no “AI platform” look before LP-09 proof  

---

## 3. Design adjectives (approved)

Use these as the art brief:

`Calm` · `Clear` · `Confident` · `Restrained` · `Operational` · `Readable` · `Human` · `Premium-without-gloss` · `Evidence-led` · `Timeless`

Avoid as brief words:

`Futuristic` · `Disruptive` · `Neon` · `Cyber` · `Playful-chaos` · `Hyper-gradient` · `AI-native aesthetic`

---

## 4. Maturity / modernity / minimalism

| Axis | Level | Meaning |
|------|-------|---------|
| **Visual maturity** | High | Feels production-ready; no prototype collage energy |
| **Modernity** | Contemporary restrained | Current, not trend-chasing (no seasonal AI look) |
| **Minimalism** | Confident minimal | Generous silence; every object earns place (Constitution) |
| **Density** | Low–medium | Evidence sections denser only where UI requires reading |

---

## 5. Product-first philosophy

1. Real product UI is the primary visual asset class.  
2. Devices and atmosphere frame — never replace — product truth.  
3. If a visual does not help the merchant recognise CartFlow’s product, remove it.  
4. Settings and admin chrome are not marketing heroes.  

---

## 6. Trust-first philosophy

1. Prefer stillness and readability over motion spectacle.  
2. Prefer honest empty/insufficient states over invented fullness.  
3. Prefer consistent product faces over “beautified” marketing UIs.  
4. Prefer whitespace over filler illustration.  
5. Never use fake charts, metrics, or logos to manufacture trust.  

---

## 7. What CartFlow should NEVER look like

- AI-themed backgrounds, brains, neural nets, glowing nodes  
- Purple-gradient “AI startup” cliché (also Brand Foundation: no parallel palette experiments)  
- Neon / cyber / sci-fi commerce  
- Loud WhatsApp-blaster marketing (green chat bubbles as brand identity)  
- Cartoon mascots / robots  
- Stock SaaS illustration packs with no product  
- Heavy glassmorphism / infinite glow  
- Decorative fake dashboards  
- Collage walls of unrelated screenshots  
- Trend-of-the-year 3D gloss that ages in 18 months  
- Fear/urgency sales aesthetics (countdown, flash, spam badges)

---

## 9. Visual consistency rules (landing)

| Rule | Direction |
|------|-----------|
| Screenshot corner radius | One consistent radius family across all product shots |
| Device framing | One phone language + one browser language — not mixed styles per section |
| Shadow | Soft, single elevation system; never theatrical drop stacks |
| Elevation hierarchy | Hero screenshot > supporting frame > atmosphere |
| Borders | Hairline/neutral when needed for UI recognition; no neon outlines |
| Background | Calm, consistent page atmosphere; no section-by-section theme hopping |
| Card hierarchy | Knowledge cards match product card language, not marketing “feature cards” |
| CTA emphasis | One primary emphasis; calm; never pulsing urgency |
| Illustration density | Sparse; zero in evidence climaxes (LP-08, prefer LP-09) |
| Section rhythm | Matches Storyboard/Wireframe silence bands |

---

## 10. Anti-patterns (explicit)

- AI-themed backgrounds  
- Excessive gradients  
- Heavy glassmorphism  
- Infinite glowing effects  
- Decorative dashboards  
- Fake analytics  
- Random floating elements  
- Multiple illustration styles on one page  
- Cartoon characters  
- Generic SaaS stock illustrations  
- Visual clutter  
- Widget purple / parallel brand experiments on landing  
- Settings screenshots as product heroes
