# Deferred visual-language weaknesses (intentional — do not fix in this task)

These are vocabulary / DNA issues for a later dedicated language phase. Signature Amplification V1 did **not** redesign them.

1. **Commerce Object glyphs remain geometric CSS approximations** of Figma signature forms (open-C, scoop, taper). They are still not production-faithful mark DNA.
2. **Evidence Density bars** still read somewhat indicator-like even when larger; a future pass may evolve field geometry without becoming charts.
3. **Living Route** still relies on a spine + nodes; Figma “living directional relationship” may need richer convergence grammar later.
4. **Momentum Trace** remains a linear step string; may need structural motion forms beyond underline progression.
5. **Quiet / Core Silence Decision Object** (empty `zone_b`) may need richer silence geometry when there is no densified decision — without inventing fake decisions.
6. **Secondary capsules** still lean text-heavy relative to primary organism.
7. **Warm hesitation signal** is scarce by rule; may need clearer semantic mapping when hesitation is the dominant truth.
8. **DS component parity** (App Bar / drawers / badges) is only lightly touched; full DS-02/03/04 fidelity is out of scope here.
9. **Mobile CO scale** is deliberately capped to avoid crowding; further mobile grammar may be needed later.
10. **Grayscale identity** still depends partly on structure that Figma would express with more distinctive silhouette language.
