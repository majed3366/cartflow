---

## Core Law

Merchants do not think in technical categories.

Merchants think in commercial situations.

Therefore, CartFlow must communicate **commercial situations** rather than independently publishing facts grouped by type.

---

## Executive Editorial Exclusivity

A commercial situation may be introduced **only once** at the executive level.

After it has been introduced:

- other surfaces may expand it,
- support it,
- execute it,
- or provide evidence,

but they must **never** restate it as a new executive message.

---

## One Commercial Situation

One Executive Brief.  
One Primary Owner.  
Many Supporting Surfaces.

Never multiple executive summaries describing the same business situation.

---

## Home

Home is an Executive Brief.

Its responsibility is to answer:

> What is happening in my business today?

It must not become:

- a dashboard,
- a report,
- a fact list,
- or a collection of unrelated observations.

Every executive card must contribute a **unique** commercial understanding.

---

## Decision Workspace

Decision Workspace owns explanation.

It answers:

> Why is this happening, and what should I do?

| Role | Law |
|------|-----|
| Home | Introduces |
| Workspace | Explains |
| Home | Never explains |
| Workspace | Never repeats Home |

---

## Supporting Surfaces

### Carts

Answer only:

> What is happening to customer carts?

### Communication

Answer only:

> What happened during communication?

Neither surface may reinterpret commercial situations.

---

## Publication Policy

Before publishing any executive statement, evaluate:

1. **Does this introduce a new commercial situation?**  
   If NO → **Suppress it.**

2. **Is this merely another wording of an already published situation?**  
   If YES → **Suppress it.**

3. **Does this increase merchant understanding?**  
   If NO → **Suppress it.**

Only unique commercial understanding may appear on Home.

---

## Editorial Authority

Executive publication is governed by **editorial exclusivity**.

Not by data availability.  
Not by counter size.  
Not by observation count.

Merchant understanding always has priority over information density.

---

## Architectural Consequence

No future architectural layer may bypass this policy.

Business Facts,  
Merchant Understanding,  
Product Intelligence,  
Knowledge Routing,  
or any future capability  

must all publish through the same **Executive Editorial Policy**.

Business Theme Engine (and any Theme-shaped synonym aggregation) is **out of the target architecture** unless a future model is proven under this law — never as a bypass.

---

## Success Test

A merchant opening Home should never feel that multiple cards are describing the same business situation.

Every card must contribute a unique understanding of the business.

If removing a card does not reduce merchant understanding, that card should not exist.

---

## Validation checklist (EEE-1…EEE-5)

Mandatory before shipping any executive-visible statement:

| # | Question |
|---|----------|
| EEE-1 | Which commercial situation does this statement introduce? |
| EEE-2 | Has that situation already been introduced at executive altitude? |
| EEE-3 | If yes — is this expand/support/evidence only (not a new executive message)? |
| EEE-4 | Does this increase merchant understanding of the store (not of CartFlow)? |
| EEE-5 | If this card were removed, would merchant understanding decrease? |

Any NO on EEE-4 or EEE-5, or unexcused YES on EEE-2 for a new executive message → **do not publish**.
