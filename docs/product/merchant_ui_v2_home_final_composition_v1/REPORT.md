## Merchant 5-second test

Without knowing Commerce Objects, a merchant should read:

1. **What is happening?** Customers leave after the shipping step (title + why).
2. **Why care?** Checkout is stalling at shipping — and CartFlow cannot yet name the cause.
3. **Does CartFlow know enough?** `الأدلة ما زالت محدودة`
4. **Act now?** No commercial change yet — keep watching until the signal clears.
5. **What next?** `راجع التفاصيل` when ready to dig deeper.

## Composition

- One primary scene (`الأهم الآن`)
- At most one silent Commerce Object mark (label visually hidden)
- Confidence in merchant Arabic (not density jargon)
- Stance block (`ماذا تفعل؟`) + one CTA
- Support aside (`لماذا يقول CartFlow ذلك؟`) — facts not repeated below
- Secondary KNOW / WATCH / LEARNING only for remaining quieter truths
- No object rail, gallery, or design vocabulary labels
- Workspace untouched
