## Cross-section message inheritance rules

1. **Single introduction:** Broader-than-recovery identity is introduced as realisation only in LP-09.  
2. **Reinforcement OK:** Recovery, evidence honesty, and purchase-stop may reappear later as support, not as new launches.  
3. **Ceiling lock:** No section may exceed its Claim Ceiling.  
4. **Evidence lock:** LP-06…LP-09 copy may not publish without coupling rules met.  
5. **CTA lock:** LP-01 and LP-15 share the same readiness-aligned primary action model.
