# Surface Compression Rules V1

**Status:** Architecture only — per-surface altitude.  
**Date (UTC):** 2026-07-28  
**Authority:** **EC-005** Shared Engine under [`EXECUTIVE_COMPRESSION_CONSTITUTION_V1.md`](./EXECUTIVE_COMPRESSION_CONSTITUTION_V1.md).  
**Non-goals:** No UI. No wireframes. No implementation.

Every surface **consumes** the same compressed decision object.  
No surface invents a parallel recommendation language or re-expands internal knowledge.

---

## 1. Universal surface law

| Law | Rule |
|-----|------|
| **One language** | What / Why / Whether now / Where (when applicable) |
| **One identity** | Same decision across surfaces; altitude differs |
| **No system tour** | Never explain CartFlow internals |
| **15-second test** | Applies to the decision unit on every surface |
| **No scroll-to-understand** | The decision unit itself must not require scroll to grasp |

---

## 2. Home

| Rule | Binding |
|------|---------|
| Altitude | **Teaser** |
| Role | Introduce the decision — continue in Workspace for commitment |
| Show | Shortest What + Why + Whether now; Where only if one locus word helps |
| Hide | Evidence, methodology, playbook validation, multi-decision essays |
| Ban | Home becoming the full executive meeting or BI strip |
| Continuity | Home language must match Workspace decision identity (Principle 7) |

---

## 3. Decision Workspace

| Rule | Binding |
|------|---------|
| Altitude | **Commit** (Primary); Next stays compact |
| Role | Executive Decision Room — commit, not report |
| Show | Full Compression Law answers; Expected result if load-bearing; methodology only when EM-001 allows and still compressed |
| Hide | Evidence chains as main content; confidence math; gate logs |
| Ban | Re-expanding into diagnostic workbook; scrolling required to understand Primary |
| Continuity | Continues Home — does not restart as a new document |

---

## 4. Knowledge

| Rule | Binding |
|------|---------|
| Altitude | **Reference** when decision-bound |
| Role | Support understanding without becoming a second decision engine |
| Show | Same executive language when linking to a decision |
| Hide | Collector / evidence-expansion internals; publication metadata |
| Ban | Knowledge inventing abstract “recommendations” outside Playbooks |

---

## 5. Daily Brief

| Rule | Binding |
|------|---------|
| Altitude | **Teaser** → short **Commit** for the day’s primary only |
| Role | Today’s executive action |
| Show | One primary decision compressed; optional next as one line each |
| Hide | Weekly analytics, multi-cause appendices |
| Ban | Brief as inbox of insights |

---

## 6. Weekly Brief

| Rule | Binding |
|------|---------|
| Altitude | **Summary** of decisions — still compressed |
| Role | Week’s committed / open decisions, not KPI magazine |
| Show | Decision outcomes + what remains actionable |
| Hide | Full evidence museums; engine performance |
| Ban | Weekly “state of the funnel” essay replacing decisions |

---

## 7. Monthly Summary

| Rule | Binding |
|------|---------|
| Altitude | **Summary** |
| Role | What was decided / resolved / still open |
| Show | Outcome-linked decisions; reality validation in one line each |
| Hide | Month-long diagnostic narrative |
| Ban | Monthly report tone that invites reading instead of knowing |

---

## 8. Notifications

| Rule | Binding |
|------|---------|
| Altitude | **Teaser** (strict) |
| Role | Pull to the decision — not deliver the workbook |
| Show | What + Whether now (and Why only if one short clause fits) |
| Hide | Where detail unless the notification *is* the locus cue |
| Ban | Pushing evidence, confidence %, or multi-paragraph explainers |

---

## 9. Future AI

| Rule | Binding |
|------|---------|
| Altitude | Same Compression Law — conversation may clarify, not dump internals |
| Role | Help the merchant decide / act in executive language |
| Show | Compressed decision answers; honest insufficiency |
| Hide | Pipeline, collectors, gate traces, “as an AI…” system narration |
| Ban | AI becoming a parallel recommendation layer outside Playbooks + Compression |

---

## 10. Surface anti-patterns (all)

| Anti-pattern | Reject |
|--------------|--------|
| Each page writes its own advice voice | Violates EC-005 |
| Workspace explains; Home contradicts | Violates Principle 7 + Shared Engine |
| Notification expands to full methodology | Violates Notification teaser |
| Knowledge lists evidence to “earn trust” | Violates Cognitive Law |
| Briefs become analytics products | Violates Compression Law |
